Privacy notice.

Last updated 27 October 2026.

Who runs this site

Shubham Sahu, Mumbai, India. This notice covers shubham-sahu.me, including its Mumbai Event Radar (/event-radar/) and Built for One (/built-for-one/) pages. Contact: shubhamsahu@nosus.foo.

What is measured

I use two analytics tools to see how many people visit and which pages they read: Google Analytics 4 (Google) and Cloudflare Web Analytics. They record the pages you view, when, roughly where in the world you are (country or city level), your browser, device and screen type, how you reached the site, and clicks on contact and résumé links. Your IP address is not stored by Google Analytics 4. I do not collect names, emails or anything you type, and the site has no accounts, forms or ads.

Cookies

Google Analytics 4 sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) in your browser to tell returning visitors apart. They expire after at most two years. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or track you across sites. Google Fonts may also be requested from Google when a page loads.

Opting out

You can block Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by turning on your browser's tracking protection or an ad blocker. The site works the same either way.

Sharing and your rights

Data is processed by Google and Cloudflare under their own privacy policies and is not sold by me. To ask about, or request deletion of, any data tied to you, email shubhamsahu@nosus.foo.
