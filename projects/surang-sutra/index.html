SURANG-SUTRA

A roll-and-fly scout and relay pucks for mine rescue.

A mine-rescue design: an X-quad inside a rotating geodesic cage, plus a cassette of five communication-only relay pucks. Shortlisted for Stage 2 of the Vishwakarma Awards 2026-27.

Design stage · no hardware built · Vishwakarma Awards 2026-27 Stage 2 shortlist

What I built

In a dark, GPS-denied mine gallery the operator link has to survive each bend, and the floor can hold debris or water.

  • SURANG is a collision-tolerant scout: an X-quad inside a single-axis rotating geodesic cage that rolls on a sound floor and flies over debris or water.
  • SUTRA is a cassette of five communication-only relay pucks. The scout drops one before each bend to keep the operator link alive in dark, GPS-denied galleries.
  • Published KiCad schematics for the relay puck and avionics interconnect (ERC clean), with a pin map, power budget and costed BOM.
  • Added a parametric FreeCAD assembly, STEP/STL exports, clearance and puck-exit checks, and cage-impact analysis with a mesh-convergence study. The analysis motivated thicker cage struts, a wider axle, softer hub caps and an offset puck cassette.
  • Design stage only: no hardware has been built or measured, and every figure is a target or a calculation. The design is not intrinsically safe and not DGMS, ATEX or IECEx certified.
  • AI tools assisted with the CAD scripting, the analysis code and the renders. The design decisions, review and fabrication are the team's.

Tools: KiCad, FreeCAD, Python, ESP32-C3, LoRa, ArduPilot.
