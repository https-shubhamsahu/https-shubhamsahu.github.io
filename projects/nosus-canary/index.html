NO SUS Canary

Different words. A traceable copy.

A Monad testnet experiment that gives each reader a differently worded note, then checks leaked text against those variants.

Testnet experiment

What I built

A leaked text copy can lose file metadata. Can the wording itself help identify its source?

  • Issued reader-specific linguistic variants and recorded opens on Monad testnet.
  • Built a paste-and-check flow for comparing leaked text with distributed variants.
  • The experiment traces a distributed copy through wording; it does not establish the identity of the person who shared it.

Tools: Monad Testnet, Solidity, Dart, Flutter, Cryptography.
