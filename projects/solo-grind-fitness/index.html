Solo Grind Fitness

A fitness tracker with an RPG progression system.

A fitness PWA experiment that organizes workouts into quests, tracks progress, and uses ranks and achievements to make training more engaging.

Deployed experiment

What I built

  • Built interfaces for workout quests, progress, ranks, and achievements.
  • Connected a web frontend to authentication and fitness tracking services.

Tools: React, TypeScript, Vite, Express, MongoDB.
