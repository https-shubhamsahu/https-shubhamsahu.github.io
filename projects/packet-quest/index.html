Packet Quest

Computer Networks revision, one mission at a time.

A browser-based Computer Networks study game by Shubham Sahu for Mumbai University BE Semester V, combining visual lessons, active recall, and exam-answer practice.

Deployed independent study aid; not an official university resource or a guarantee of exam performance.

What I built

Recognizing a networking definition is different from explaining it in an exam. Packet Quest connects visual concepts to challenge questions and writing checklists.

  • Organized 24 question-bank missions across Units 4, 5, and 6, including addressing, routing, TCP/UDP, DNS, and application-layer concepts.
  • Paired visual lessons with active-recall challenges, including choices, matching, ordering, and numerical answers.
  • Added mistake review, exam-writing checklists, a four-hour study plan, and a 20-mark mock.
  • Kept progress in browser storage and connected the creator welcome to my portfolio and NO SUS.

Tools: JavaScript, HTML, CSS, SVG, Browser local storage.
