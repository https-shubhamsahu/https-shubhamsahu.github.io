VibeSplit

Group expenses, settled without the spreadsheet.

A group-expense tracker by Shubham Sahu for trips, shared bills, and everyday spending with friends. Members can join through a shareable link and track who paid for what.

Deployed web application

What I built

  • Built trip creation, member invitations, expense logging, and itemized splits.
  • Implemented settlement calculations, guest participation, trip history, and theme controls.

Tools: React, React Router, Firebase Authentication, Firestore.
