M-Indicator 2.0

A new front door for a familiar commute.

A responsive website concept for M-Indicator, bringing transit features, city coverage, and product information into one browsing experience.

Independent website concept · M-Indicator belongs to Mobond

What I built

Transit information spans routes, fares, cities, and safety. The website needed a clear way to explore that breadth.

  • Built a responsive multi-page frontend with an interactive Leaflet city map.
  • Organized transit features, news, product updates, and contact pages with shared navigation.
  • The recording shows my website work; the original transit app, brand, and adoption figures belong to Mobond.

Tools: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Leaflet.js.
